Projects

Most of the projects that I worked on during my life cannot be shown for various reasons, but here is a small list with descriptions of the projects that I can remember.

  • CT Scans & 3D Medical Visualisation (Apprenticeship)

    During my apprenticeship, I primarily focused on processing CT scans: displaying them and reconstructing 3D models. My development work was mainly in C++, with significant time dedicated to debugging the Boost libraries. I handled various aspects of medical technology, with emphasis on 3D visualization and rotational analysis. I used Microsoft Kinect for motion tracking; with OpenCV, I generated a skeletal overlay to detect and record the user's posture.

  • Bachelor Thesis: X-ray Device Positioning

    I dedicated my bachelor thesis to the reconstruction of X-ray device positioning during surgical operations. Surgeons often need to reproduce the exact same angle utilized in previous scans (such as CTs), but since medical equipment is frequently moved within the operating room, capturing the image from the identical spatial perspective is difficult. I addressed this using the Solaris Cam, which offers a cost-effective solution for tracking and alignment.

  • Machine Learning Algorithm to Predict Type 2 Diabetes (Master's)

    During my master's studies, I worked on a machine learning algorithm to predict Type 2 Diabetes. This project involved analyzing medical and demographic data to identify patterns and risk factors associated with the development of Type 2 Diabetes.

    • Analyzed medical and demographic data to identify risk factors
    • Built and evaluated predictive models
    • Dataset: Kaggle
  • Predicting Spoilers in Movie Reviews

    Another project I worked on involved predicting whether a movie review contains spoilers. This task required natural language processing techniques to analyze the text of movie reviews and determine if they contained spoilers.

    • Applied NLP techniques to analyze review text
    • Classification task: spoiler detection
    • Dataset: Kaggle
